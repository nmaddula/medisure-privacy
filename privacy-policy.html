MediSure Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MediSure (“the app”) helps you manage medicine schedules and reminders for yourself and family profiles. This policy describes what information the app handles and how.

Information the app stores

The app stores the following on your device only (local storage):

  • Profile names and relationship labels (e.g. self, parent)
  • Medicine names, dose instructions, and schedule settings
  • Records of scheduled doses, taken/skipped status, and snooze state

We do not operate a cloud server that receives this data in the current version of the app. Data is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Permissions

The app may request device permissions so reminders work reliably, including:

  • Notifications and exact alarms — to alert you at scheduled times
  • Full-screen alerts and optional display over other apps — to show an alarm-style screen when the app is not open
  • Restart after reboot — to restore scheduled reminders

You can change or revoke permissions in Android system settings at any time.

Backups

If you enable Android backup for your device, the operating system may include app data in encrypted device backups according to Google’s backup policies. That is controlled by your device settings, not by MediSure servers.

Children

The app is intended for caregivers managing medicines. Profiles may represent family members; all data remains on the device.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions about MediSure, contact the developer via the support email listed on the app’s Google Play store listing.
